# 02 — Brand and Business Strategy

> **Owner of:** positioning, brand rules, claims policy, conversion strategy.

## 1. Positioning

**Brand:** Mohd Mufeet – Freelance Full Stack Developer.
**Positioning statement (internal, not public copy):** A personal, developer-led alternative to agencies: clients work directly with the person who builds their product.

Brand attributes: **direct, honest, technically credible, clear.** Tone: confident without hype; plain language; first person ("I").

## 2. Brand rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Write in first person singular ("I build…") | Use "we", "our team", "our agency" |
| Present Mohd Mufeet as the individual developer | Invent a company name, logo-as-company, employees, or office |
| Lead with development; show projects early | Make the site look like a digital marketing agency |
| State only verified facts | Invent statistics, testimonials, client names, results, years of experience |
| Mark future services Coming Soon or hide them | Advertise SEO, ads, social media marketing as available |
| Describe own role precisely (see §3) | Claim authorship of work done by others |

## 3. Claims policy (critical)

1. **KAP Controls backend accuracy.** Mohd Mufeet did **not** create the original Node.js/Express.js backend. Existing backend functionality was **migrated** from Node.js/Express.js to Supabase per project requirements, under senior developer guidance. He also worked on **frontend UI implementation and integration**. Public copy may say this (approved wording in `07` §7). It must never say or imply he architected, designed, or developed the original backend.
2. **No unverified metrics.** No numbers of clients, projects, users, performance gains, revenue, or satisfaction.
3. **No expertise levels or years.** Skills are listed as "technologies I work with," without percentage bars, star ratings, or "expert/advanced" labels, unless the owner supplies verified wording (OQ-10).
4. **Project facts.** Only verified facts appear. Unverified fields in a case study are omitted from the page (not filled with plausible text).
5. **Testimonials.** Only genuine, permitted feedback with the author's consent. Otherwise the section is hidden.
6. **Contradiction check.** The blog project (`blog-for-client-fhod.vercel.app`) and KAP Controls are treated as unrelated unless the owner confirms (OQ-23).

## 4. Target clients (hypothesis, A-02)

Small and growing businesses, startups, and individuals needing a business website, e-commerce store, or full stack web application. Used only to shape tone and page order.

## 7. Conversion strategy

**Primary CTA:** "Let's Work Together" → `/contact` (or `#contact` on Home).
**Secondary CTA:** "Explore My Work" → `/projects` (or `#projects` on Home).

Funnel: Land (Hero) → Understand (Services, Skills) → Believe (Projects, Experience, About) → Act (Contact). Each Home section ends with a path forward; the navbar always exposes the primary CTA. Service cards pre-select that service in the contact form (see `09`, `10`).

Success measures (counted, not targeted — no invented goals): number of enquiries received, enquiry-to-conversation rate, which service/project led to the enquiry, and top traffic sources. Collection method is defined in `10` §10 and `11`.

## 8. Future expansion rules

- Development remains the visible primary identity at all times.
- New service categories activate through data (`status` field in `09`), not redesign.
- A future category appears only when the owner can actually deliver it, and then only after content, project references, and SEO metadata are approved.
- If marketing services launch, they appear under a clearly separate category label below development; the Hero and nav copy remain development-first.

## 9. Differentiation

Personal accountability, modern stack, transparency about role and scope, and a lean site that practises what it sells (fast, accessible, well-built). Do not compare against named competitors.
