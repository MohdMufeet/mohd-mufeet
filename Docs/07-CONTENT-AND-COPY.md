# 07 — Content and Copy

> **Owner of:** all public copy and content rules.
> Status tags: **APPROVED** (supplied by owner), **DRAFT** (proposed; needs owner confirmation before launch), **MISSING** (owner must supply). Agents may implement DRAFT copy for development but must list every DRAFT/MISSING item in the pre-launch checklist (`14` §12). Copy is stored in config modules, not hard-coded in components.

## 1. Voice

First person singular, plain English, specific and calm. Short sentences. No buzzword stacking ("synergy", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary"). No exclamation marks. No unverifiable superlatives.

## 8. Work process (DRAFT; OQ-18)

1. **Discuss** — We talk through your goals, audience, and requirements.
2. **Plan** — I outline scope, pages/features, and the approach.
3. **Design & Build** — I build the interface and functionality, sharing progress along the way.
4. **Review** — You review the work and I refine it based on feedback.
5. **Launch** — I deploy the project and hand over what you need to run it.

Do **not** add post-launch support, warranties, timelines, or revision limits unless the owner supplies them.
